The Petito Family · Whiting, New Jersey

A Family Shop With a Firehouse Heart

"We saw a demand for quality leather gear at a fair price — with the options first responders actually want. So we built it ourselves."

JP Custom Leatherworks started in 2015 with an idea at the kitchen table and launched its first store in 2016. Today, Joe Petito and his four kids still cut, stamp, stitch, pack, and ship every order together — bringing American manufacturing back, one strap at a time.

Our mission: build gear with pride, purpose, and uncompromising craftsmanship — gear first responders can trust with their lives.
