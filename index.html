The Petito Family · Whiting, New Jersey

The Family Behind The Gear

"We saw a demand for quality leather gear at a fair price — with the options first responders actually want. So we built it ourselves."

JP Custom Leatherworks started in 2015 with an idea at the kitchen table and launched its first store in 2016. Today, Joe Petito and his four kids still cut, stamp, stitch, pack, and ship every order together.

The goal hasn't changed since day one: quality gear at a fair price, made the right way.
