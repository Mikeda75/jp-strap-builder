About The Truck Belt

Built from 12 oz full-grain leather, our Truck Belt is designed for serious, heavy-duty use. Each belt is custom made to your exact size and crafted at a 2" width to provide the strength, stability, and comfort needed to securely support your essential tools.

Every belt features a rugged Cobra® buckle and is assembled with solid copper rivets for maximum durability and long-term performance in demanding environments.

Available add-on options include an axe ring, box light holder, and a general-purpose D-ring for carabiner attachments. Custom attachments can also be added upon request, allowing you to build a belt tailored to your specific needs and workflow.

How to Measure

Measurements should be taken while wearing bunker gear, using a cloth measuring tape.

To measure yourself, take a soft, flexible measuring tape and measure around the spot where the belt will sit—usually just above your hips. Make sure the tape is snug but not tight. If you don’t have a soft tape measure, no problem! You can use a piece of string or even a belt you already have. Wrap that around the same area, mark where it meets, then lay it flat and measure that length with a ruler.
