About The Dog Collar

JP Custom Leatherworks dog collar’s are made with your pup in mind. All collars are made with 8-10oz. English bridal leather and are equipped with a heavy duty welded D-ring to attach a leash and tags. We can paint the letters of your collar but if your pup scratch's you may want to consider just doing an outline to prevent the paint from being scratched off.
