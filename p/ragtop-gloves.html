About The RAGTOP Gloves

The RagTop Extrication Mitts are your go-to lightweight, breathable, cut-resistant and low-profile firefighter auto extrication / technical rescue gloves. With an A5 cut rating, these gloves boast a simple design and practical level of protection for your hands, all while maintaining a flexible and comfortable fit. Ultimate dexterity and superior durability with no trade-offs

  • Premium materials
  • ANSI level 5 cut rating
  • 100% Kevlar outer shell
  • Black Kevlar full inner layer
  • Synthetic leather palm w/ kangaroo leather panels
  • Kevlar laminate on back of fingers
  • Ribbed padded knuckles
  • Lightweight and breathable
  • Ergonomic build
