About The Quick Ship Custom Glove Strap

Our Quick Ship Series is designed to give you the customization you want, without the wait!

All Custom Quick Ship items ship within 1 week, typically within 48 hours! 

**NO PAINT OR CUSTOM STAMPS**

JP Custom Leatherworks glove strap’s  are built to endure the extreme conditions that firefighters deal with everyday. Our glove strap’s are designed to hold extrication or structural firefighting gloves. All straps are made with 1" wide english bridle leather and equipped with 2 heavy duty snaps to ensure that your gloves will stay in place but remain easily accessible. Please be sure to include text in appropriate field if you would like your glove strap personalized. If you have any questions, please reach out to us!
