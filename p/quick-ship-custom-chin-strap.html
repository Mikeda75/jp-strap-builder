About The Quick Ship Custom Chin strap

Our Quick Ship Series is designed to give you the customization you want, without the wait!

All Custom Quick Ship items ship within 1 week, typically within 48 hours! 

**NO PAINT OR CUSTOM STAMPS**

JP Custom Leatherworks Chin straps are made with 8-10oz english bridle leather that will hold up to a careers worth of abuse. Straps are easily installed by removing 1 chicago screw from each side, slipping the leather through the mounting ring on your helmet, and reinstalling the Chicago screws. If you have any questions about our chin straps, feel free to send us an email.
Please be sure to include text in appropriate field if you would like your chin strap personalized. If you have any questions, please reach out to us!
