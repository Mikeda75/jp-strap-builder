About The JP Large Tote Bag

JP Custom Leatherworks Full Grain Leather Tote

Built for real life and made to last, the JP Custom Leatherworks Tote is handcrafted entirely in house by our design team using premium full grain leather. Every detail is intentional. From the sturdy construction to the clean, timeless design, resulting in a bag that works just as hard as you do.

This tote features full leather shoulder straps, heavy-duty hardware, and double-stitched seams throughout to ensure long-term durability and reliability. Designed for busy days and life on the move, it offers ample interior space to comfortably hold any laptop with room to spare, along with everything else you need for a short trip or day-to-day carry.

Perfect for teachers, moms, professionals, or anyone looking for a tough, dependable bag without sacrificing style.

Features:

  • Handcrafted from premium full-grain leather

  • Made 100% in-house by the JP Custom Leatherworks team

  • Full leather shoulder straps

  • Heavy-duty hardware

  • Double-stitched seams for added strength and longevity

  • Spacious interior fits all laptops with room to spare

  • Choice of 0, 1, or 2 interior pockets

  • Interior leather ring for keys or accessories

This is a tote designed to age beautifully, perform daily, and stand the test of time.
