About The Pocket Size Leather Notebook Cover

Leather Notebook Cover with Field Notes Book

Our handmade leather notesbook cover is a great addition to your everyday carry essentials. Whether you are a first responder, business executive or just someone who needs a place to write down random thoughts, this one is for you! Our notebook cover features a hidden pocket and a half pocket. Cover comes with a Ruled Field Notes Brand 3.5" x 3.5"notebook.  Like all of our other products, we only use the highest quality materials. Each cover is cutout by hand, hand-sewn (with Tiger Thread), and all edges burnished to a mirror like finish to endure years of use. 

  • Leather Cover measures 4"x6"
  • 1 Hidden Pocket
  • 1 Half Pocket
  • Hand-Sewn with Tiger Thread
  • Made with only the best Leather (from Tasman Leather Group located in Hartland, Maine)
  • Replaceable Field Notes Book
  • Lifetime Warranty
