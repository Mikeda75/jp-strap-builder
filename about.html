The JP Story

It Started At The Kitchen Table

The idea for JP Custom Leatherworks came in 2015, when we noticed there weren't many companies offering a quality leather product with the design flexibility first responders want. In September 2016 we took the leap — ordered every tool, machine, and side of leather we needed — and spent months learning the craft through articles, forums, videos, and a ton of practice.

In November 2016 we launched our first online store. We've been growing faster than we ever expected since.

My name is Joe Petito. I'm a father to four amazing kids, and we all work together to get every order filled, packed, and shipped. Thank you for taking the time to learn who we are — and thank you for your support.
